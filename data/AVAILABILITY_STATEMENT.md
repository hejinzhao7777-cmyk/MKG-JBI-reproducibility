# Manuscript-ready availability statement

## Data Availability

The data analysed in this study are publicly available. TCGA molecular and clinical data were obtained from the NCI Genomic Data Commons (https://portal.gdc.cancer.gov/). Independent validation cohorts were obtained from the Gene Expression Omnibus under accession numbers GSE31210, GSE14520, GSE29609, GSE39582, GSE84437, GSE65858, GSE50081 and GSE76427. METABRIC molecular and clinical profiles were accessed from the public cBioPortal Datahub study `brca_metabric`, whose database is distributed under the Open Data Commons Open Database License. Participant-level matrices are not redistributed. The accession-level source index, preparation hashes, locked analysis outputs, and manuscript source tables are available in the accompanying repository at `https://github.com/hejinzhao7777-cmyk/MKG-CMPB-reproducibility`.

## Code Availability

Scripts for the core locked analyses, leakage audits, and METABRIC preparation
and portability audit, together with the locked configuration, provenance
manifests, result files, and manuscript source tables, are available at
`https://github.com/hejinzhao7777-cmyk/MKG-CMPB-reproducibility`.
